About

An AI Agent Builder Immersion

AgentForge was a three-day, zero-to-deployed workshop for students who wanted to go beyond chatting with AI and start building with it. Over three back-to-back days, participants worked hands-on to design, build and deploy their own AI agents.

Organised by the SPEC Innovation and Incubation Center together with the Department of Computer Science & Engineering, it brought together 184 students from first year through final year across five departments.

  • Hands-on from the first session — every participant builds
  • From idea to a deployed, working agent in three days
  • Open to all years and branches — no prior agent experience needed

The journey

Build. Deploy. Grow.

  1. Day 01 · 6 Oct

    Build

    Foundations of AI agents — how they reason, use tools and remember. Set up the toolkit and build a first working agent.

  2. Day 02 · 7 Oct

    Deploy

    Connect agents to real data and APIs, make them reliable, and take them from the laptop to a live, shareable deployment.

  3. Day 03 · 8 Oct

    Grow

    Refine, extend and present. Teams demo what they built and leave with a working agent and a path to keep going.
